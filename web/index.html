A tiny expressive playground

Make your eyebrows dance.

Your hands puppeteer a pair of cartoon brows on top of your face. Open wide for a happy arch, pinch for a sad little U, and make them disagree to look gloriously confused. Each mood has its own sound and emoji.

Runs entirely in your browser. Your video is never uploaded.

😄

🤨

Confused

One brow up, one down. They disagree.

boing

How it works

  1. 1 · Track. MediaPipe finds your face and both hands in the camera image.
  2. 2 · Map. Thumb-to-finger distance sets a brow's height and shape. Hand tilt rotates it. Hand spacing moves the brows apart.
  3. 3 · React. The two brows together are read as a mood, which plays a sound and bounces out emoji.
